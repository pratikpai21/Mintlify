---
title: "Reports"
description: "See how quickly your team replies."
---

Admins open reports with **📊 Reports**. They show:

- **Reply times**: how long customers wait for a first answer.
- **Replies per person**: who is answering how much.
- **Promises kept and missed**: from [Follow up](/inbox/urgent-and-follow-up).
- **AI help used**: summaries and suggested replies this month.

Use them in your weekly team catch-up: who needs help, and when customers wait longest.
