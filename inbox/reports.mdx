---
title: "Reports"
description: "See how quickly your team replies, when customers write, and what they write about."
---

Admins tap **Reports** on the left bar (on a phone, **Reports** in the tab bar at the bottom). Reports opens as its own page. Tap **Chats** to go back, or on a phone, the Back button.

## Choose what to look at

- **All numbers** adds up every WhatsApp number. Pick one number from the same list to see just that one.
- **Today**, **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days** sets the period.
- **Download** saves a spreadsheet file with the numbers for each day and for each person.

## What you see

- **Customer messages**, **Typical first reply** and **Answered within 1 hour**, each with a short note comparing them with the same length of time just before. For example, "up 12% on the 7 days before" or "was 18 min".
- **Customer wrote last**: chats where the customer's message is still the last one.
- **Missed calls**: customer calls nobody picked up. See [Calls](/inbox/calls).
- **Messages per day**: customer messages and your replies, as a chart. Tap **Show as a table** for the numbers.
- **Chats by label**: how many of the chats in the period carry each label.
- **Busiest hours**: the days of the week and the hours of the day, darker where more customers wrote. The busiest hour is named at the top.
- **Team**: replies sent, chats, how fast each person answers first, and their promises kept, late or missed.
- **Promises not followed up within 4 hours**: from [Follow up](/inbox/urgent-and-follow-up). Tap one to open that chat.
- **AI help**: summaries and suggested replies used in the period.

Use Reports in your weekly team catch-up: who needs help, and when to have more people answering.
