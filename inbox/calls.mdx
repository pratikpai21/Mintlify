title: "Calls"
description: "See every customer call in the chat, and never lose a missed call."
---

When a customer calls your WhatsApp number, you still answer on the phone. If you like, the inbox can keep track of every call too, so the whole team knows who called and nobody is forgotten. Everything on this page starts switched off until an admin turns it on (see Settings below).

## While a customer is calling

A green banner appears at the top of the inbox for everyone who can see that customer's chat, for example "📞 Asha is calling. Pick up on the phone." Tap the banner to open the chat. It goes away when the call ends.

The banner only shows when an admin has switched on **Call alerts in the inbox** (see Settings below).

## Calls in the chat

When an admin has switched on **Show calls in chats**, a line shows in the customer's chat when a call ends:

- **📞 Missed voice call**: nobody picked up.
- **📞 Voice call declined**: WhatsApp reported the call as declined.
- **📞 Voice call, answered on the phone · 3 min**: someone answered, and how long it lasted.

Video calls say "video call" instead.

## A missed call counts like a new message

With calls shown in chats, a missed or declined call:

- opens the chat again if it was closed, for the person who gets new chats,
- shows as unread,
- sends the chat's owner a notification, if call alerts are switched on,
- counts as a customer waiting for a reply.

A call answered on the phone only moves the chat to the top. Nobody is waiting for a reply.

In **Reports** you see how many calls were missed, in total and per day.

## Settings (admins)

Open **Settings**, then **Calls**.

- **Show calls in chats**: a line in the customer's chat for each call. A missed call opens the chat again and counts in Reports. Switched off, calls don't show in the chats at all, including calls from before.
- **Call alerts in the inbox**: the "calling now" banner and a notification to the chat's owner after a missed call.
- **Send a message after a missed call**: works even when calls aren't shown in chats. The inbox sends your message right after a missed call, for example "Sorry we missed your call. Please write your question here and we'll reply soon." You can change the wording. The same customer gets it at most once every 6 hours. It shows as **🤖 Automatic**, and the chat still waits for a reply from your team.
- **Decline calls straight away**: for businesses that only work by chat. Calls are declined as soon as they ring. With calls shown in chats, they count as missed.

Tap **Save** when you're done. All four are off until an admin switches them on.

<Note>Calls are answered on the phone. The inbox doesn't place calls, and it can't answer them in the browser.</Note>
