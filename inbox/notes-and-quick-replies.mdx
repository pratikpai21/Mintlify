## Internal notes

Under the reply box, switch from **Reply to customer** to **Internal note**. Notes show inside the chat for your team only. **The customer never sees them.**

Type **@** and a name to tag someone. They get a notification, and they can see the chat to help, even if they don't own it.

<Tip>
  Use notes for things like "Customer called, sending a replacement" so whoever opens the chat next knows what happened.
</Tip>

## Quick replies

Save answers you type again and again: delivery times, return policy, payment details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create them">
    Your initials → **Quick replies** → **Add a quick reply**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use them">
    In the reply box, type **/** and pick one from the list. Change anything you like before sending.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Add a picture

A quick reply can also send a picture, like a size chart, price list or payment QR code. The message goes out as the text under the picture.

1. In **Quick replies**, add or edit one and tap **🖼 Add picture**. Use a JPG, PNG or WebP photo up to 5 MB.
2. When you pick that quick reply with **/**, the picture is attached and the message is in the reply box. Change either, or tap **✕** to send the text only, then press **Send**.

The message can be left empty if you only want to send the picture. Pictures are sent in replies only, never in internal notes.
