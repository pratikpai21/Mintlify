---
title: "Reactions, copy and forward"
description: "React with an emoji, copy a message, or send it on to another chat."
---

Every message has a small menu, just like WhatsApp:

- **On a phone:** press and hold the message.
- **On a computer:** point at the message and click the small **⌄** arrow in its corner, or right-click the message.

The menu has a row of quick reactions on top, then:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Reply** | Answer this message, quoted |
| **Internal note** | Write a note to your team about it |
| **Copy** | Copy the message's words |
| **Forward** | Send it on to another chat |
| **Select more** | Pick several messages to copy or forward together |

<Tip>
On a phone, **swipe a message to the right** to reply to it straight away.
</Tip>

## Reactions

When a customer reacts to a message (👍 ❤️ 🙏 …), you see the emoji under that message, just like on the phone. Hover over it to see who reacted.

To react yourself, open the message's menu and tap 👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 🙏 or ✅. The customer sees it on their phone straight away. Tap the same emoji again to take it back. Your team sees your name on the reaction.

A reaction doesn't move the chat to the top and doesn't count as a reply, so a customer who was waiting for an answer is still waiting.

## Emojis in a reply

Tap **😊** at the left of the reply box to open the emoji panel, then tap the emojis to add. They go where your cursor is, in a reply or an internal note. On a phone, you can also use your keyboard's emojis as usual.

## Copy a message

Choose **Copy** in the message's menu, then paste the words anywhere. For a photo or document, this copies its caption. To send the photo itself on, use **Forward** instead.

## Forward a message

1. Choose **Forward** in the message's menu. For a group of photos sent together, this forwards all of them.
2. Search for and tick up to **5 chats**.
3. Tap **Forward**.

Each one arrives as a new message from your number, with photos, videos, voice notes and documents included. The customer doesn't see where it came from.

<Note>
To keep your number safe, you can only forward to chats where the customer has written to you before. See [Safe use of WhatsApp](/account/safe-whatsapp-use).
</Note>

## Several messages at once

1. Choose **Select more** in a message's menu, or tap **⋯** at the top of the chat, then **☑️ Select messages**.
2. Tap each other message you want. A tick shows on the ones you picked. Tap one again to untick it.
3. At the bottom, choose:
   - **📋 Copy** copies them all, oldest first, each with its time and who wrote it.
   - **↪ Forward** sends them, in order, to up to 5 chats. You can forward up to 20 messages at a time.
4. **Cancel** takes you back to the reply box.

Internal notes can be copied but never forwarded, so they always stay inside your team.
