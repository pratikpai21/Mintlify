## 🚩 Urgent

Anyone on a chat can flag it with **🚩** at the top of the chat. An urgent chat:

- gets a red **🚩 Urgent** tag that everyone sees,
- shows in the **Urgent** filter, which counts how many are waiting (newest flag first),
- sends an alert to the chat's owner and to admins.

Closing the chat clears the flag.

The main list keeps showing the newest messages first, so a new customer is never hidden under flagged chats.

With [AI help](/ai/ai-help) on, an admin can also let the AI flag urgent chats by itself. If someone clears a flag the AI set, the AI leaves that chat alone for a day.

## No reply 1h

**No reply 1h** marks chats where the customer has waited more than an hour for an answer.

## Follow up

When someone on your team promises something like "will check and update" and the chat then goes quiet for **4 hours**, the chat is marked **Follow up**. Find them all with the **Follow up** filter. Closing the chat clears it.

Reports show how many promises were kept and how many were missed.

## Notifications

[Turn on notifications](/get-started/on-your-phone) on each device to hear about new messages in your chats, tags, urgent chats, and (for admins) a WhatsApp number dropping off.
