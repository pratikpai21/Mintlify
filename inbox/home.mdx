Tap **Home** on the left bar (on a phone, **Home** in the tab bar at the bottom). Home opens as its own page and covers all your WhatsApp numbers. Tap **Chats** or **Open inbox** to go back to your chats, just as you left them. On a phone, the Back button does the same. Tap **↻** to count again.

## Today

Everyone sees four boxes, counting open chats you can see:

- **Unread**: chats waiting for you.
- **No reply for 1 hour**: customers who have waited more than an hour.
- **🚩 Urgent**: chats flagged [urgent](/inbox/urgent-and-follow-up).
- **Follow up**: promises like "will check and update" that still need an answer.

Tap a box to see those chats in your list. If you have more than one number, each box shows how many are on each number, and the list opens on the number that has them.

If [call lines](/inbox/calls) are switched on, admins also see **Missed calls** today.

## Waiting on us

The customers who wrote last and are still waiting for a reply, on all your numbers, longest wait first. The wait turns orange after 1 hour and red after 4. Tap a name to open the chat.

## Returns board

With the [courier returns](/add-ons/courier-returns) add-on, admins and people with **↩️ Returns** switched on see every return in four columns:

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Booked** | Pickups not done yet, with where they stand (for example "Pickup failed, they'll try again") |
| **On the way back** | Picked up and coming back to you |
| **At the warehouse** | Back with you and waiting for a refund or replacement: what the customer wants, how long it has waited, and buttons to **Refund**, **Send replacement** or mark it **Already done** |
| **Done this week** | Refunded, replacement sent or dealt with in the last 7 days |

Each column shows the first three, then how many more. Tap a card to open that customer's chat. The button at the top, like **2 returns to decide**, jumps to the board. On a phone, the columns sit one under another, with **At the warehouse** first.

Below the board:

- **Needs a call**: pickups or deliveries that failed, and parcels lost or coming back.
- **Returns this month**: see [Courier returns](/add-ons/courier-returns#numbers-for-the-month).

The red number on the Home button counts the returns and calls waiting.

## Team today

Admins also see how the day is going so far: customer messages, replies sent, the average time to a first reply, and a bar for each person's replies. For more, tap **Open reports**.
