oneCommerce puts your business WhatsApp number on one shared screen. Your whole team answers customers from there, everyone can see who is handling which chat, and nothing gets missed.

## What you can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Answer as a team" icon="users" href="/inbox/who-answers">
    Every chat has an owner. Hand chats over, tag a colleague, close when done.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Never miss a customer" icon="bell" href="/inbox/urgent-and-follow-up">
    Urgent flags, "No reply 1h", and reminders for promises like "will check and update".
  </Card>
  <Card title="See Shopify orders" icon="bag-shopping" href="/shopify/orders-and-products">
    Orders, payment and tracking right next to each chat.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI help" icon="sparkles" href="/ai/ai-help">
    Chat summaries and suggested replies in one click.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Set up in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Go to [app.onecommerce.tech](https://app.onecommerce.tech) and type your Business ID. [How to log in](/get-started/log-in)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your WhatsApp number">
    Scan a QR code from the phone with your WhatsApp Business number. [Link WhatsApp](/get-started/link-whatsapp)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team">
    Give each person their own login. [Add your team](/get-started/add-your-team)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get it on your phone">
    Install the inbox as an app and turn on notifications. [On your phone](/get-started/on-your-phone)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Inside the inbox, click your initials (bottom left) → **❓ Help and getting started** for a short version of this guide.
</Tip>
