---
title: "Log in"
description: "Open your inbox with your Business ID, username and password."
---

Everyone logs in at the same address: **[app.onecommerce.tech](https://app.onecommerce.tech)**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type your Business ID">
    It's in the welcome message you received from oneCommerce. Tap **Continue**. Your device remembers it next time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your username and password">
    Admins get theirs from oneCommerce. Team members get theirs from an admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Two-step code (if you turned it on)">
    Type the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. [About two-step login](/account/security)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I forgot my password">
    Ask an admin of your business. In **Team and passwords** they tap **New password** next to your name and send you the new one.

    If you are the only admin, [contact us](/help/contact). We'll check it's really you first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I lost the phone with my authenticator app">
    Type one of your saved backup codes instead of the 6-digit code. Or ask another admin to switch two-step login off for you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm seeing someone else's business">
    Tap **Switch business** under the login button and type the right Business ID.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My business has its own address">
    Some businesses have their own inbox address (for example `wa.yourshop.com`). If you were given one, you can use it instead. Both open the same inbox.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
