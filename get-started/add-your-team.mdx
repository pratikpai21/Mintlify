## Add a person

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team">
    Click **Settings** on the left bar (on a phone, the **Settings** tab at the bottom) → **Team and passwords**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them">
    Type their name, choose a role, then tap **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send them their login">
    The inbox shows their username and password **once**. Send both to them, along with your Business ID.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Admins and team members

| | Admin | Team member |
|---|---|---|
| Sees chats | Every chat | Chats they own, and chats they're tagged into |
| Links WhatsApp numbers | ✅ | — |
| Adds people, labels, settings | ✅ | — |
| Reports | ✅ | — |
| Refunds, returns | ✅ | Only if an admin switches it on for them |

## Choices per person

In **Team**, next to each person, admins can switch on:

- **Gets new chats**: this person is the front desk. New chats go to them first.
- **AI help**: shows the ✨ Summarise and ✨ Suggest buttons to this person.
- **💸 Refunds**: lets them cancel and refund Shopify orders (when cancel and refund is on).
- **↩️ Returns**: lets them book courier return pickups (with the Courier returns add-on).
- Whether they may see and copy full customer numbers.

## When someone leaves

Tap **New password** next to their name straight away. Their old password stops working at once. Team also shows when each person last logged in, and from which device.

How many people you can add depends on your [plan](/account/plans-and-credits).
