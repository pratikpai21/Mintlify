---
title: "Use it on your phone"
description: "Install the inbox as an app and get notifications."
---

The inbox works in any browser. On a phone, install it as an app so it opens full screen and can send you notifications.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. Open your inbox in **Safari**.
    2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
    3. From now on, open it from that icon.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open your inbox in **Chrome**.
    2. Tap your initials → **Install as an app on this device** (or Chrome's **⋮** → **Install app**).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Turn on notifications

Tap your initials → **Turn on notifications**, and allow them when asked. You'll be told about:

- new messages in chats you own,
- someone tagging you in a note,
- chats flagged 🚩 Urgent (owners and admins),
- a WhatsApp number dropping off (admins).

<Tip>
  On a phone, the keyboard's **Enter** makes a new line. Only the **Send** button sends, so you won't send half a message by accident.
</Tip>
