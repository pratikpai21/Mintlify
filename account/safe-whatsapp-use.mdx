---
title: "Safe use of WhatsApp"
description: "Simple habits that keep your number in good standing."
---

The inbox links to your number by QR code, the same way WhatsApp Web does. This is **not an official WhatsApp product**, and WhatsApp can limit or ban numbers that break its rules. You accepted a short note about this when you linked your number.

Most businesses never have a problem. These habits keep it that way:

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Only reply to people who wrote to you" icon="reply">
    The inbox can't start new chats with customers, on purpose.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No bulk-sending tools" icon="ban">
    Never send bulk or promotional messages from this number with any other tool. WhatsApp's own Business broadcasts in the phone app are fine, sent sparingly and only to customers who know you. See [Broadcasts](/inbox/broadcasts).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep replies personal and useful" icon="heart">
    Many customers blocking or reporting a number is the quickest way to a ban.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep the phone in use" icon="mobile">
    Open WhatsApp on the phone at least once every 14 days, or the link drops off.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The inbox also waits a moment between messages it sends, so your number never looks like it's sending in bulk. It only sends to people who have written to you first: that's why replies, reactions and forwarded messages can only go to chats the customer started.
