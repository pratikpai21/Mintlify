## Two-step login

With two-step login, logging in needs your password **and** a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone (like Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator). We recommend it for every admin.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Your initials → **🔐 Two-step login**, and scan the code with your authenticator app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your backup codes">
    You'll get a few one-time backup codes. Keep them somewhere safe, away from your phone. Each one works once if you lose your phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Lost your phone and your backup codes? Ask another admin to switch two-step login off for you in **Team**.

## Customer numbers are hidden

Full customer phone numbers are hidden on screen, so they can't be copied down by anyone looking over a shoulder. **👁** shows a number for a few seconds and **⧉** copies it. In **Team**, admins choose whether team members may do this.

## Who logged in

**Team** shows when each person last logged in and from what device. Admins get an alert when someone logs in from a new device.

## When someone leaves

Give them a **New password** in Team straight away. Their old password stops working at once.

## Your data

Your chats are kept for your business only, and backed up every night. See our [privacy policy](https://onecommerce.tech/privacy.html) for the details.
