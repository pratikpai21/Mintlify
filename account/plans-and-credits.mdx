Your plan sets:

- how many **WhatsApp numbers** you can link,
- how many **people** can log in,
- how many **AI credits** you get each month,
- whether **Shopify** and **automatic AI checks** are included.

See all plans and prices on our [pricing page](https://onecommerce.tech/#price).

## AI credits

One credit is **one summary or one suggested reply**. Credits are shared by the whole business and start fresh each month. **AI settings** shows how many are left this month.

## Reaching a limit

If you try to add a number or a person beyond your plan, or run out of AI credits, the inbox tells you. Tap **Message oneCommerce on WhatsApp** in that message, and we'll add an extra or move you to a bigger plan.

You can add extras without changing plan: an extra number, an extra person, or 1,000 more AI credits.

## Add-ons

Add-ons are extras for particular needs, like [Website enquiries](/add-ons/website-enquiries) and [Courier returns](/add-ons/courier-returns). [Contact us](/help/contact) to add one.
