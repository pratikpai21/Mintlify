<Info>
  The order tracking reply comes with the **Business** plan, and needs [Shopify connected](/shopify/connect).
</Info>

Customers ask "where is my order?" all day, in many languages: "mera order kab aayega", "माझी ऑर्डर कुठे आहे", "parcel status?". With the order tracking reply switched on, the inbox answers these by itself, so your team can spend its time on the chats that need a person.

## What the customer gets

A few seconds after the customer stops typing, the inbox reads their message. If it's a plain "where is my order" question, it looks up their **latest order** in your store by their WhatsApp number and replies with:

- the **order number**,
- **how it's going**: being packed, shipped, on the way or out for delivery,
- the **tracking link**: the courier's link when there is one, otherwise your store's order status page.

In the chat, the reply shows as **🤖 Automatic**. It doesn't count as a reply from your team, and it doesn't move the chat or give it to anyone.

## When it leaves it to your team

The inbox sends nothing, and your team answers as usual, when:

- the customer is also complaining (damaged or wrong item, a refund, very late, upset),
- the chat is marked urgent or already has the Complaint label,
- someone on the team has already replied,
- the latest order shows as **delivered**, was **cancelled**, or has a delivery problem,
- there's no order in the last 60 days for that number, or the store can't be reached right then.

In most of these cases a short note in the chat tells the team why, for example "🤖 Asked where their order is: order #1042 shows as delivered. Over to the team."

Each chat gets the automatic reply at most **once every 12 hours**, so a customer who asks twice isn't sent the same message twice.

## Set it up (admins)

1. Open **Settings** → **Shopify**.
2. Under **Order tracking reply**, write your reply. Use these blanks:
   - **\{order\}**: the order number
   - **\{status\}**: how it's going
   - **\{link\}**: the tracking link (this one must be there)
3. Tick **Order tracking reply** to switch it on, then tap **Save order tracking reply**.

It's off until you switch it on. Untick it and save to stop it at any time.

<Tip>
  Write the reply in the language most of your customers use. Every customer gets the same words.
</Tip>
