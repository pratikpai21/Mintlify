<Info>
  The order tracking reply comes with the **Business** plan, and needs [Shopify connected](/shopify/connect).
</Info>

Customers ask about their orders all day, in many languages: "where is my order?", "mera order kab aayega", "माझी ऑर्डर कुठे आहे", "not received yet", "any update?". With the order tracking reply switched on, the inbox answers every one of these by itself, straight away. Your team can then spend its time on the chats that really need a person.

## What the customer gets

A few seconds after the customer stops typing, the inbox reads their messages and finds their order:

- by the **order number** they typed (like #1042), even if it was ordered from another phone number,
- otherwise by their **WhatsApp number**: their latest order from the last 60 days.

Then it replies with one of three messages, all written by you:

| The customer… | They get |
|---|---|
| asks where their order is, or just sends their order number | your **reply**: order number, how it's going and the tracking link |
| says it hasn't arrived, asks for "any update", or is upset about the wait | your **late order reply**: an apology, with the same order details |
| needs a person: damaged or wrong item, refund or return, "it shows delivered but I didn't get it", a cancelled order, or no order could be found | your **holding message**, and the team gets a note explaining why |

"How it's going" is one of: being packed, shipped, on the way, out for delivery. The tracking link is the courier's when there is one, otherwise your store's order status page.

**Orders not from your store?** If you've connected your courier company for [courier returns](/add-ons/courier-returns), the inbox also finds orders you sent with the courier directly.

In the chat, every automatic message shows as **🤖 Automatic**. It doesn't count as a reply from your team, and it doesn't move the chat or give it to anyone.

## Your team still sees everything

- Complaint labels and the urgent flag still go on. The customer gets their answer at once, and the team still knows to follow up.
- Whenever the inbox sends the holding message instead of the order status, a 🤖 note in the chat says why, for example "order #1042 shows as delivered" or "no order for this number".
- If someone on the team has already replied after the customer's last message, the inbox stays quiet and leaves it to them.
- The same answer isn't sent twice within 12 hours. If the customer asks again and nothing has changed, the team gets a note instead. If the order has moved on (for example, it's now out for delivery), the new status goes out.

## Set it up (admins)

1. Open **Settings** → **Shopify**.
2. Under **Order tracking reply**, check the three messages. Each comes with ready-made wording. In the reply and the late order reply, use these blanks:
   - **\{order\}**: the order number
   - **\{status\}**: how it's going
   - **\{link\}**: the tracking link (this one must be there)
3. Tick **Order tracking reply** to switch it on, then tap **Save order tracking reply**.

It's off until you switch it on. Untick it and save to stop it at any time.

<Tip>
  Write the messages in the language most of your customers use. Every customer gets the same words.
</Tip>
