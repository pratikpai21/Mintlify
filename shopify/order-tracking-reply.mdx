<Info>
  The order tracking reply comes with the **Business** plan, and needs [Shopify connected](/shopify/connect).
</Info>

Customers ask about their orders all day, in many languages: "where is my order?", "mera order kab aayega", "माझी ऑर्डर कुठे आहे", "not received yet", "any update?". With the order tracking reply switched on, the inbox answers every one of these by itself, straight away. Your team can then spend its time on the chats that really need a person.

## What the customer gets

A few seconds after the customer stops typing, the inbox reads their messages and finds their order:

- by the **order number** they typed (like #1042), even if it was ordered from another phone number,
- otherwise by their **WhatsApp number**: their latest order from the last 60 days.

Then it replies with a message written by you. The reply steps up with each day the customer asks about the same order:

| When | They get |
|---|---|
| **Day 1**: the first time they ask | the order number, how it's going and the tracking link |
| **Day 2**: they ask again, or say it hasn't arrived, ask for "any update", or are upset about the wait | an apology, with the same order details |
| **Day 3**: they ask again | "We have raised it with our delivery partner and are chasing it today." |
| **Day 4 and after** | "It is now with our senior team, and we are chasing the delivery partner every day." |

Some orders get their own message instead:

| The order… | They get |
|---|---|
| has a delivery problem (a failed or missed delivery) | a "sorry, it's held up" message saying you're checking; from day 3, the day 3 and day 4 messages |
| shows as delivered, but they're asking about it | "it shows as delivered; we're checking"; from day 3, the day 3 and day 4 messages |
| has come back to you | "it has come back to us; our team will message you" |
| isn't shipped yet | "it's being prepared for dispatch" |
| can't be found | a request to send their order number |

When only a person can help (a damaged or wrong item, a refund or return, a cancelled order), the team gets a note explaining why, and the customer gets your **holding message** if you switched it on.

The inbox only ever replies when the customer writes to you. It never sends these messages by itself.

"How it's going" is one of: being packed, shipped, on the way, out for delivery. The tracking link is the courier's when there is one, otherwise your store's order status page.

**Sent the order again?** If you used **Send again** after an order came back (with [courier returns](/add-ons/courier-returns)), the reply is about the new parcel: its status and its tracking link, not the old one that came back.

**Orders not from your store?** If you've connected your courier company for [courier returns](/add-ons/courier-returns), the inbox also finds orders you sent with the courier directly.

In the chat, every automatic message shows as **🤖 Automatic**. It doesn't count as a reply from your team, and it doesn't move the chat or give it to anyone.

## Your team still sees everything

- Complaint labels and the urgent flag still go on. The customer gets their answer at once, and the team still knows to follow up.
- The holding message is off unless you tick **Send a holding message when a person needs to answer**. With it off, the customer gets no automatic message in these cases and your team answers.
- Whenever an order needs a look, a 🤖 note in the chat says why, for example "order #1042 shows as delivered" or "no order for this number".
- If the automatic check ever stops working, order questions are still answered: the inbox spots them by their words ("order", "parcel", "kab aayega", "ऑर्डर कुठे"…) and sends the same replies. The chat gets a 🤖 note saying so, admins get a notification, and Home shows a warning until it works again.
- If someone on the team has already replied after the customer's last message, the inbox stays quiet and leaves it to them.
- A plain "Ok", "Yes", "Thanks" or 👍 is an answer to your team, not a question, so it gets no automatic reply.
- The customer gets at most one message a day for the same thing. If they ask again the same day and nothing has changed, the team gets a note instead. If the order has moved on (for example, it's now out for delivery), the new status goes out.

## Escalate delivery problems to your courier (optional)

If you share a WhatsApp group with your courier company, the inbox can chase late deliveries there for you. When a customer asks again on another day, says their order hasn't arrived, or the order shows a delivery problem (failed delivery, "delivered" but they didn't get it, returned), one of your numbers posts in that group and tags the people you choose:

- the order number and its **AWB** (tracking number) and courier,
- how it's going, when it was ordered and how many days ago,
- the delivery city and pincode,
- what the customer says is happening, in a line or two (for example "courier called but never came; needs it by Friday"),
- on how many days the customer has asked, and since when.

The post never includes the customer's name or phone number. Each order is posted at most once a day. If the customer chases again on a later day, the post says "2nd follow-up", and so on. Orders that haven't shipped yet have no AWB, so they aren't posted. The chat gets a 🤖 note either way, so your team knows what happened.

To set it up, first follow the group in **WhatsApp groups**. Then, under **Escalate delivery problems to a WhatsApp group** in **Settings** → **Shopify**, pick the number that posts, the group and the people to tag, tick the box and tap **Save escalation**.

## Set it up (admins)

1. Open **Settings** → **Shopify**.
2. Under **Order tracking reply**, check the messages: **Replies by day** (day 1 to day 4) and **Special cases**. Each comes with ready-made wording. Use these blanks:
   - **\{order\}**: the order number
   - **\{status\}**: how it's going
   - **\{link\}**: the tracking link (it must be in the day 1 and day 2 messages; elsewhere, a line with it is left out when there's no link yet)
3. Tick **Order tracking reply** to switch it on, then tap **Save order tracking reply**.

It's off until you switch it on. Untick it and save to stop it at any time.

<Tip>
  Write the messages in the language most of your customers use. Every customer gets the same words.
</Tip>
