## The Orders panel

Tap **Orders** at the top of a chat. You'll see that customer's Shopify orders, newest first:

- the items, and what was paid,
- payment status, and anything refunded,
- delivery progress, with the courier's latest status,
- the tracking link.

Tap **Send tracking link** to put the tracking link in your reply box, ready to send.

Can't find the order? Search the panel by **order number** or **phone number**. Useful when a customer writes from a different number.

<Note>
  Viewing orders never changes anything in Shopify. Only [cancel and refund](/shopify/cancel-and-refund) does, and that is off until an admin turns it on.
</Note>

## Shopify customers in the list

Customers who have ordered from your store get Shopify's **bag icon** in the chat list, and the **Shopify** quick filter shows only them. New chats are checked in the background, so the icon may take a little while to appear.

## Products: send a product link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Products">
    In the Orders panel, switch to **Products**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the product">
    Type part of the product name. Results come live from your store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put it in your reply">
    Tap **Put in reply**. A short message with the product's web link is ready in the reply box. Change it if you like, then send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Admins can set the wording around the link: your initials → **Shopify** → **Products tab: Put in reply**.
