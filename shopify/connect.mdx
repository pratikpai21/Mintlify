Shopify is optional, and included in the Business plan (and the earlier Growth and Pro plans). Connecting needs an **admin** of your inbox, and someone who can approve apps in your Shopify store.

## The quick way: an install link

oneCommerce makes a small Shopify app for your store and sends you an **install link**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to both">
    Make sure you're logged in to your inbox and to your Shopify admin in the same browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the link">
    Open the install link we sent you. Check the permissions Shopify shows and tap **Install**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    You come back to your inbox, connected. Nothing to copy or paste.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also start from the inbox: **Settings** → **Shopify**, type your store address (`yourstore.myshopify.com`) and tap **Connect Shopify**.

<Note>
  Use your **myshopify.com** address (you'll find it in Shopify admin → Settings → Domains), not your shop's own web address.
</Note>

## Why the permissions

The app asks Shopify for permission to **read** orders, customers, deliveries and products, so the inbox can show them next to chats. It asks to **change** orders only so admins can cancel and refund from the inbox, and that stays switched off until an admin turns it on. [Cancel and refund](/shopify/cancel-and-refund)

## Disconnecting

**Settings** → **Shopify** → **Disconnect**. Orders stop showing next to chats until you connect again. You can also remove the app in Shopify admin → Apps.
