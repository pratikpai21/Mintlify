<Info>
  Courier returns is an **add-on**, and needs [Shopify connected](/shopify/connect) and an account with a supported courier company. [Contact us](/help/contact) to add it, and to ask which courier companies are supported.
</Info>

## Set it up (admins)

Open **Settings** → **Returns** and fill in:

- your **courier company** and your account details with them,
- the **warehouses** returns go back to (the first is the main one),
- the **message for the customer** when a pickup is booked.

Admins can always book. To let a team member book too, switch on **↩️ Returns** for them in **Team**.

## Book a return pickup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the order">
    In the chat, open **Orders** and find the shipped order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap ↩️ Return">
    The form is filled in from Shopify: items, address, weight, and the warehouse it shipped from. Pick the reason and what the customer wants (a refund or a replacement), check it and book.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the customer the message">
    A message with the tracking link is ready in the reply box. Check it and send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The inbox then follows the return and adds a note to the chat when the parcel is **picked up**, and again when it's **back at your warehouse**. When it's back, everyone who books returns also gets a notification, and the return waits on **🏠 Home** until someone refunds or replaces. You can cancel a return until it's picked up.

## Home: returns to deal with

**Home** is on the left bar (on a phone, in the tab bar at the bottom). The returns part is for admins and people with **↩️ Returns** switched on. Its red count shows how many things are waiting. It covers all your WhatsApp numbers.

**Back at the warehouse: refund or replace?** lists every returned parcel that's back with you, oldest first. Each one shows the order, the customer, the items, the reason, what the customer wants, and how long it's been waiting (red after 2 days). On each one:

- **💸 Refund** opens the customer's chat with the refund ready for the returned items. Check the amount and refund. (Only for people who can refund.)
- **🔄 Send replacement** opens the replacement form in the customer's chat.
- **Open chat** takes you to the conversation, to check with the customer first.
- **Already done** clears a return you dealt with another way, for example a refund by bank transfer.

The button for what the customer asked for is green. Refunding the order, or sending a replacement for it, takes the return off Home by itself, even if you do it from the order card in the chat. Every step leaves a note in the chat.

**Needs a call** lists problems on the way that a call may fix: a pickup that failed, a parcel that failed the courier's check at pickup, a delivery that failed, a shipment coming back undelivered, or a parcel the courier lost. Call the customer (or your courier company), then tap **Already done**. If something new goes wrong with the same parcel, it shows again.

## Numbers for the month

Home also shows **Returns and refunds** for the current month:

- **Returns booked**, parcels **back at the warehouse**, and **replacements sent**.
- **Refunded from the inbox**: money refunded with **Refund** or **Cancel** in the inbox this month (refunds made directly in Shopify aren't counted).
- **Time to decide**: on average, how long returns waited at the warehouse before a refund or replacement.
- **Top reasons** and **most returned products**, so you can spot a product or packing problem early.

## Send an order again (after RTO)

When a **prepaid** order comes back undelivered, **🔁 Ship again** shows on its order card. It books the same parcel out again with your courier, without a second Shopify order, and gives you the label and tracking.

## Send an order again (lost by the courier)

When your courier marks an order as **Lost**, **🔁 Send again** shows on its order card, for any payment method. It books a new parcel to the customer, without a second Shopify order, and gives you the label and tracking.

- **Prepaid orders** go out prepaid.
- **Cash on delivery and part-paid orders** go out as cash on delivery, and the courier collects only what the customer still owes. The form shows the amount before you book.

Refunded orders don't get the button. If the parcel sent again is lost too, **🔁 Send again** comes back.

## Send a replacement

For a delivered order, **🔄 Send replacement** books a replacement shipment. Adjust your stock in Shopify by hand.

## Ask the courier to bring an order back (RTO)

When an order is stuck, misplaced or refused on its way, you can ask your courier company to mark it RTO (return to origin) and send it back to you. You then get it back to reuse, or, if the courier loses it, you can claim the money from them.

1. Open the customer's order in the Orders panel. On a shipped order that isn't delivered yet, tap **🔙 Ask courier to RTO**.
2. Pick why it should come back (stuck, misplaced, customer refused, or other) and add a few words if you like.
3. Tap **Ask for RTO**.

The inbox posts the request in the WhatsApp group you share with your courier: the one chosen under **Escalate delivery problems to a WhatsApp group** in **Settings** → **Shopify**, tagging the same people. The post has the order number, tracking number and reason, and never the customer's name or number. If you already asked in the group yourself, tick **Already asked in the group** and the inbox only keeps track of it. Nothing is sent to the customer.

From then on, the inbox checks with the courier and moves the request along by itself, with a note in the chat each time:

| Stage | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Asked for RTO** | waiting for the courier to mark it | **Chase again** (a follow-up post in the group, once a day), **Mark lost**, **Withdraw** |
| **Coming back** | the courier marked it RTO | Chase again, Mark lost |
| **Back with you** | it reached you | **Received OK (reuse)** or **Received damaged** |
| **Lost by the courier** | the courier lost it | **Claim raised**, with the amount (the order's value is filled in) |
| **Claim raised** | waiting for the courier to pay | **Money received**, with the amount paid |

If it's delivered to the customer after all, the request closes by itself.

All requests are in the **RTO requests** list in the Courier tab. **Home** shows the ones that need you: not marked RTO 2 days after asking, still not back 10 days after being marked, back and not checked yet, lost with no claim, or a claim not paid after 15 days. The month's numbers on Home add how many were asked, came back and were lost, and how much was claimed and paid.

### RTO asks typed in your courier groups

Often you ask the courier for RTO by simply typing in your WhatsApp group with them. The inbox can pick those up too, so every request is in one list.

1. Go to **Shopify settings → Pick up RTO requests from courier groups** (admins).
2. Tick the courier groups to watch. Groups on all your numbers are listed; the group you chose for delivery escalation starts ticked.
3. Tick your staff among the group members. Messages from your own WhatsApp numbers always count; the courier's people never do.
4. Tap **Save and look back**. The inbox goes through the messages it already has in those groups and tells you how many RTO requests it added.

From then on, whenever you or a ticked staff member write **RTO** with an order number (like #1042) or a tracking number, the request is added and followed like any other. Several orders in one message become several requests. "Don't RTO" or "cancel RTO" is skipped. Nothing is posted in the group or sent to the customer; the customer's chat gets a 🔙 note.

### Reconcile with the courier

At the top of **RTO requests** in the Courier tab:

- **Stage chips** (All, Asked, Coming back, Back, Lost, Claim raised, Closed), each with its count. Tap one to see only those.
- **Totals** for what's on the list: how many were asked, came back and were lost, how much was claimed, how much was received and how much is still pending.
- **⬇ Download sheet** gives a spreadsheet of every request (or only the stage you're looking at): order, tracking number, customer, reason, when and how it was asked, stage, the courier's status, dates back or lost, claim and payment amounts and dates, and the outcome. Use it to check against the courier's own statement.

Closed requests stay on the list for 90 days, and always in the sheet.

## The Courier tab

The **Courier** tab in the Orders panel shows everything booked from the inbox in four lists: **Returns**, **Replacements**, **Sent again** and **RTO requests**, with each one's latest status. Returns that are back show whether they were refunded or replaced, or are still waiting.
