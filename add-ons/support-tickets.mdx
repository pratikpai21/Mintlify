<Info>
  Support tickets is an **add-on**. It works with or without Shopify. [Contact us](/help/contact) to add it.
</Info>

A ticket follows **one problem on one order**: a damaged item, a missing part, something that doesn't work. It stays open, with someone's name on it, until it is sorted out, so nothing is forgotten in a long chat.

## Set it up (admins)

Open **Settings** → **Tickets** and fill in:

- **Problems customers report**: your own list, such as "Damaged" or "Part missing". The list starts empty; add the ones you hear most.
- **Late after**: how many hours a ticket may stay in each stage before it shows as late. Use 0 for no limit.
- **Who looks after what**: your support person, your workshop person and your manager.

To stop offering a problem, tap **Take off the list**. Old tickets keep it.

### Switch tickets on or off

At the top of **Settings** → **Tickets**, tick **Use tickets in this inbox**. It starts off, so you can set everything up and tell your team first: while it is off, nobody sees tickets, and no forms, messages or reminders go out.

When you tick it, the Tickets tab and page appear for everyone straight away. Untick it to hide them again. Tickets already made are kept and come back when you switch it on.

## Open a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer's chat">
    Open **Orders** next to the chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 🎫 Ticket on the order">
    Or open the **Tickets** tab and tap **🎫 New ticket**, for a problem that isn't about an order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what the problem is">
    Pick the problem, the item, what the customer wants, and how it came in. Tap **Open ticket**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The ticket gets a number, such as **T-12**, and a line appears in the chat so everyone there knows.

The same item on the same order can't have two open tickets. If one is already open, you are shown that one.

## Let the customer open the ticket

Instead of filling it in yourself, send the customer their own form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Tickets tab in the chat">
    Tap **send the ticket form**. A message with the customer's own link appears in your reply box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it and press Send">
    The customer opens the link, picks their order and the problem, describes it and adds photos or a short video.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The ticket opens by itself">
    It arrives with your support person, with a line in the chat. The customer's photos are on the ticket under **From the customer**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The same link then shows the customer where their ticket stands, in plain words. They don't see names, your notes or what the workshop found.

A link stops working a week after it was sent if it wasn't used, and a month after its ticket is closed.

### Send the form automatically (admins)

In **Settings** → **Tickets**, tick **Send the form by itself when a customer reports a problem with a product**. This needs [AI help](/ai/ai-help) with automatic checks switched on.

When a customer writes about a faulty, damaged, wrong or incomplete product they received, the inbox sends your message with their link, once. It shows in the chat as **🤖 Automatic**, with a line for the team.

It is not sent:

- for an order that is late or hasn't arrived,
- in a WhatsApp group,
- while a ticket is already open in that chat,
- more than once a day to the same customer.

You can change the message in the same place. Keep the link marker that is already in it (the word link inside curly brackets): that becomes the customer's own link.

<Tip>
  For the first week, read the 🤖 lines in your chats to see that the form goes to the right customers. If it doesn't suit you, untick the box and send the form by hand.
</Tip>

## The stages

| Stage | What is happening | Who has it |
|---|---|---|
| **New** | Just reported | Support |
| **Waiting for customer** | You asked for photos or details | Support |
| **Coming back** | The item is on its way back to you | Support |
| **At workshop** | The item is being checked | Workshop |
| **Fix in progress** | The replacement, refund or repair is being done | Support |
| **Closed** | Sorted out | |

Each stage has a time limit. A ticket past its limit shows **Late** in red.

## Tickets move by themselves

You don't need to change the stage. You do the real step from the order, and the ticket follows. This works with the courier returns add-on.

| You do this | The ticket moves to |
|---|---|
| Book the return pickup (**↩️ Return** on the order) | **Coming back** |
| The courier delivers it back to you | **At workshop**, with your workshop person |
| The workshop saves the fault found | **Fix in progress**, back with support |
| Send the replacement or the missing part (**🔄 Send replacement**) | **Fix in progress** |
| The courier delivers it to the customer | **Closed**: replacement sent, part sent, or repaired |
| Refund the item (**💸 Refund**) | **Closed**: refunded |
| The return pickup is cancelled or fails | Back to **New**, for you to decide |
| The customer writes back while you were waiting for them | Back to **New** |

**What the customer wants sets the path.** A missing part is simply sent, with no pickup. A replacement, repair or refund comes back to be checked first.

### Do the next step from the ticket

Each open ticket shows a button for what comes next: **↩️ Book return pickup**, **🔄 Send the part**, **🔄 Send replacement** or **💸 Refund**. The form opens on top of the ticket, already filled in with the ticket's item, the customer's address and what they want. Check it and book. You stay on the ticket, which has already moved on. The message for the customer waits in your reply box.

There is no button while the courier has the item, or while something for that item is already on its way. Only people allowed to book returns or refund see these buttons.

Each ticket says under its stage what will move it next. Automatic moves show as **Automatic** in its history, with a line in the chat.

A ticket is only moved by its own item: refunding one item on an order doesn't close a ticket about another item.

<Tip>
  You can still change the stage by hand, for something done outside the inbox. For example, a customer who sends the item back themselves.
</Tip>

## Tell the customer at every stage (admins)

In **Settings** → **Tickets**, tick **Send the customer a message when their ticket opens, moves to another stage or closes**.

The customer then gets one short WhatsApp message each time, with their ticket number and their own link to see where it stands. It shows in the chat as **🤖 Automatic**. For example: "Ticket T-12: your battery has reached us and is being checked."

- Each stage has its own message. Change the wording, or untick a stage to send nothing for it.
- In a message you can use the ticket number, the item and order, how it was sorted out and the customer's link. The words in curly brackets in each message show where these go; keep them as they are.
- The message goes a minute after the change. If you change a stage by mistake and change it back straight away, nothing is sent.
- It only goes to customers who wrote to you, and never in a WhatsApp group.
- If it can't be sent (for example your number is disconnected), the ticket's history says so and shows the message, so you can send it another way.

<Tip>
  Read the six messages before you switch this on. They go to every customer with a ticket.
</Tip>

## Work on a ticket

Open the **Tickets** tab next to the chat and tap the ticket.

- **Stage**: moves by itself (see above). Change it only for something done outside the inbox. It goes to the person who looks after that stage, and the time limit starts again.
- **With**: hand it to someone by name. They can then see the chat too.
- **Fault found**: the workshop writes what was wrong.
- **Add a note** for the team. Notes stay on the ticket.
- **History** shows everything that happened, with who and when.

Changes are saved as you make them.

## Close a ticket

Most tickets close by themselves. To close one yourself, at the bottom of the ticket pick **how it was sorted out** (replacement sent, refunded, repaired, part sent, no fault found and so on), add a note if you like, and tap **Close ticket**.

Closed by mistake, or the problem came back? Open the ticket and tap **Reopen**.

## All tickets in one place

Tap **Tickets** on the left bar (on a phone, in the bar at the bottom). Every open ticket is there:

- **Board** shows a column per stage, so you see what is waiting where. **List** shows them one under the other, late ones first.
- **Open**, **Late**, **Mine** and **Closed** narrow it down. Closed tickets stay for a month.
- Search by ticket number, order, customer, item or problem.
- Tap a ticket to open it over its chat, with its buttons for the next step.

The red number on the Tickets button is how many are late. **Home** also shows a Tickets card with the late ones, yours first.

When a ticket goes late, whoever has it and your manager get a notification, once for each stage.

## Who can see tickets

A ticket belongs to the customer's chat. Whoever can see the chat can see and work on its tickets.
