---
title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Quick fixes for the most common problems."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My WhatsApp number shows Scan QR">
    The link to your phone dropped off, usually because WhatsApp wasn't opened on the phone for 14 days, or the device was removed under **Linked devices**. Click **Scan QR** and scan again from the phone. [Steps](/get-started/link-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A message is stuck on sending">
    If WhatsApp has just reconnected, the inbox waits a few seconds and sends it. If it still doesn't go, check the number isn't showing **Scan QR**, then try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A team member can't see a chat">
    Team members only see chats they own or are tagged into. Hand the chat over to them, or tag them in an internal note.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm not getting notifications">
    Tap your initials → **Turn on notifications** on that device, and allow them. On iPhone, notifications only work when the inbox is [added to the Home Screen](/get-started/on-your-phone) and opened from there. Also check your phone isn't on Do Not Disturb.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Orders don't show next to a chat">
    Check Shopify is connected (your initials → **Shopify**). If the customer ordered with a different number, search the Orders panel by order number.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The ✨ buttons are missing">
    An admin needs to switch on AI help for you in **Team**. If they're there but don't work, your business may have used this month's AI credits. [Plans and credits](/account/plans-and-credits)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I forgot my password">
    Ask an admin for a **New password**. If you're the only admin, [contact us](/help/contact).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Still stuck? [Contact us](/help/contact).
